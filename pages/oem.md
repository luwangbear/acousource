Following is the general process for our OEM service.

1. Customer Inquiry
: We receive physical samples, reference images, specifications or drawings from the customer.

1. Feasibility Evaluation
: Our engineering team analyzes the product design, materials, and technical requirements to determine production feasibility.

1. Quotation & Lead-time Estimation
: We consult with our long-term suppliers to provide cost and production lead-time estimates.

1. Sample Development
: We purchase parts from suppliers and develop samples. We conduct electro-acoustic performance testing using professional measurement systems such as DAAS to make sure the sample meets the required specifications.

1. Sample Refinement & Approval
: We send samples to the customer for evaluation. Adjustments and improvements are made based on feedback until final approval.

1. Order Confirmation & Deposit Payment
: The customer places the official order and provides a deposit to confirm production.

1. Material Procurement
: We purchase parts from suppliers for mass production.

1. Mass Production & Quality Control
: We conduct incoming material inspection, mass production and final testing. We run a 100% sweep test for all products to ensure quality and consistency.

1. Packaging
: We provide custom color box or carton packaging according to customer requirements.

1. Shipping
: We arrange shipment through freight forwarding partners, or deliver to customer’s designated logistics providers. 

1. After-Sales Support
: We provide continuous after-sales support to assist with any inquiries, technical questions, or additional requirements.
