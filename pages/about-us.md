### About AcouSource Audio  
<br>

Products
: AcouSource Audio has been designing and manufacturing speaker components since 2000. We specialize in the design and production of reliable, cost-effective speaker drivers, including woofers, subwoofers, midrange drivers, and tweeters. Our products are used in a wide range of applications, including professional live sound, line arrays, PA systems, fixed installations, karaoke, and home entertainment.

Services
: We provide flexible ODM/OEM services tailored to customer requirements, whether based on technical specifications, physical samples, or reference designs. Backed by engineers and production staff with more than 20 years of industry experience, our team supports customers through every stage, from product evaluation and sample development to mass production. We place strong emphasis on quality control and production consistency to ensure every product meets customer expectations. We also offer professional and fluent English customer service to ensure clear and efficient communication.

Markets
: With over two decades of continuous operation, we have built long-term relationships with customers around the world, with primary markets in North America, Latin America, and Africa. Over the years, we have developed extensive experience working with international clients, including brand owners, distributors, and private-label companies.

Logistics
: Located in Jiashan County, Zhejiang Province, China, AcouSource Audio benefits from proximity to Shanghai Port and Ningbo Port, ensuring efficient, flexible, and reliable global logistics.

<br>
We are committed to building long-term partnerships with clients worldwide by providing practical manufacturing solutions, stable product quality, and reliable service.
